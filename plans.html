Frequently asked questions

Still unsure which plan fits? Email support@buildsync.ph.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. The difference is prorated on your next monthly bill.

How do I pay?

GCash, Maya, BDO, BPI, Metrobank, Landbank, Visa or Mastercard. This demo takes no real payment; only the confirmation email is real.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Plans renew monthly and you can cancel whenever you like. Cancel within 7 days of payment and 80% is refunded; a 20% cancellation fee applies.

Is my project data secure?

Uploads use 256-bit SSL encryption, and access is limited to the roles you assign. We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act.

What is included in the dashboard demo?

The public demo shows project metrics, daily logs, photo uploads and team roles with sample data. It runs in your browser and resets when you reload.
